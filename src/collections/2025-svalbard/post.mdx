> "It was a grandly desolate, sublime, weird landscape, utterly barren and unlike anything I had ever seen."

&ndash; John M. Longyear

He said it best. This place really evokes the feeling of standing at the edge of the world.
It feels like this should be an unexplored section of the map titled "There be dragons", but instead
it's a cozy, modern little village with hotels, restaurants, and even a grocery store! Oh and guns. Lots of guns.

Svalbard was much better than I expected. Lots of interesting people up here.
I hope to come back someday to experience the dark season.
